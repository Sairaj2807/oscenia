Before you start

4 things to know

🧪
It's a demonstration. The clients, projects and figures are realistic sample data — nothing is connected to a live database yet. Refreshing the page resets everything. That's expected.
👤
Switch who you are. Top-right has a role switcher — view as CEO, Sales & Marketing, or Warehouse & Ops. Each role sees only its relevant screens.
🌐
Two languages. The EN / ID toggle switches the interface between English and Bahasa Indonesia.
⚡
The big idea. Everything hangs off one Project ID, and a status change in one place auto-triggers the next step elsewhere. Watch for the small "auto" pop-ups — that's the system working on its own.
The overall flow

How a job moves through the system

A single event travels left-to-right through the whole business — without anyone re-typing the same information twice.

QUOTE → DOWN PAYMENT → PROJECT CREATED → DESIGN → APPROVAL → VENDORS
→ EXECUTION → CLIENT FEEDBACK → CREW PAID → BOOKS UPDATED
Screen by screen

What each screen does

The screens below follow the same order as the flow — from the first quote to the closed, paid, accounted-for event.

1Dashboard

CEO

The company at a glance — pipeline value, average event margin, money owed to you, active projects, with revenue and margin charts.

Look for: the right top-level numbers to open the system to.

2Quotations & MoU

Sales

Where every deal begins. Draft a quotation, send it, and on acceptance the system generates the MoU/contract and hands Finance a ready deal.

Try: New Quotation, or Mark accepted on a sent quote — download the MoU PDF.

3Finance & Invoicing

CEO / Sales

Installment invoicing (DP / progress / final) and the key trigger: confirming the down payment.

Try: Confirm Down Payment → watch the "auto" cascade create the project, start the pipeline & update the books.

4Projects — Pipeline Board

Everyone

Your production workflow across the seven milestones, with a Timeline (Gantt) tab filterable by division.

Look for: whether these seven stages match how your projects really flow.

5Project Detail

Everyone

One project up close — milestone progress, design files with version control & approval, and a threaded discussion with @mentions.

6Project P&L

CEO / Sales

A live profit-and-loss for a single event — revenue, cost breakdown and margin, kept separate from office overheads.

7Accounting

CEO

The books, generated automatically — double-entry journal, Income Statement and Balance Sheet built from daily transactions.

8Budgeting

CEO

Spending ceilings per category vs. actual, with an automatic red-flag when a category goes over budget.

Try: Log unplanned cost → watch a category breach and raise an alarm.

9Profit Simulator

CEO

A "what-if" tool — drag sliders for contract value, vendor cost, crew, and watch margin, profit and break-even recalculate live.

10CRM & Clients

CEO / Sales

Your client database — lifetime value, preferences, satisfaction, birthdays.

Try: click any row for the Client 360 — profile, interaction history, vendors used & per-event ratings.

11Automated Cron

CEO / Sales

Background jobs that run daily on their own — notably scanning for client birthdays and alerting Sales ahead of time with ready WhatsApp/Email drafts.

Try: Run daily jobs → Preview draft to see the message.

12Client Survey

Client-facing

The post-event rating form (1–10). The client's score feeds crew bonuses automatically.

Reach it via Crew & Incentives → "Open client survey".

13Vendors

CEO

Approved suppliers, a 3-vendor cost comparison that flags the lowest quote, a third-party equipment catalog with live availability, and 1–5 scorecards.

14Procurement & Petty Cash

CEO / Warehouse

Expense requests through Draft → Pending → Approved → Paid, with proof-of-payment upload and an auto-deducting petty-cash balance.

15Warehouse

CEO / Warehouse

Dual-logic inventory — consumables (used up → cost) vs fixed assets (rented gear that goes in-transit and is checked back in) — plus signed delivery orders.

Try: Check in an in-transit item to return it to the warehouse.

16Crew & Incentives

CEO

Performance-based crew pay — bonuses from the client's post-event rating × each crew member's base rate.

17Sales Report

CEO / Sales

A leaderboard of the sales team by closed deals, with automatic commission calculations.

If you only have 5 minutes

A suggested path

  1. Quotations → accept a quote → Finance → Confirm Down Payment (watch the auto-notifications)
  2. Projects → see the new project appear → click advance a couple of times
  3. CRM → open a client's 360 view
  4. Automated Cron → Run daily jobs → Preview a message draft
  5. Flip the EN / ID toggle and the role switcher to see how it adapts
