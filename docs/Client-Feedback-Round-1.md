## Open questions

Asked one at a time; answers recorded here.

1. **W3 — how should approval work when someone submits on another person's behalf?** — **Decided (1 Oct):** only the assignee can press Submit. PM and leaders can still Start, Block or reassign, but not submit for someone; the PM can then always give the final approval.
2. **CH1 — replace the thread panel with quoted replies, or keep both?** — **Decided (1 Oct): keep both.** "Reply" posts a quoted reply at the bottom of the main chat; "Reply in thread" keeps the side panel for longer side-conversations.
3. **C1 — a ticked contact without marketing consent: exclude with a warning, or allow?** — *parking for client.* Building the default: excluded, with a warning showing how many were skipped.
4. **V1 — how the five pipeline stages map to the existing PO statuses.** — **Decided (1 Oct):** accounting statuses stay untouched; add a separate delivery status (Not started / In progress / Delivered). Board: Approved = PO Issued · DP Paid · In Progress · Delivered/Completed · Settled = Fully Paid. Draft and Cancelled sit in a filter, off the board.
5. **C2 — what the Google Calendar section should do.** — *parking for client.* Building the default: simulated "Connect Google Calendar" plus a calendar view showing CRM dates (events, nudges, birthdays, anniversaries) beside Google events.
6. **CH2 — do calls come with one-to-one (direct) messages?** — *parking for client.* Building the default: add direct messages as well.
7. **J2 — which date drives the reports today; confirm the "date received" rule.** — **Checked (1 Oct):** already the rule. REQ-01 (commit 67851c6) keeps the document's own date beside the accounting date (= date recorded/received), and every report uses the accounting date. **Gaps:** the two dates are only on invoice issue / vendor invoice, not on the DP-received and vendor-payment pop-ups; and those actions need the backend, so the offline demo never shows them.
